The recommendation weighs ownership status, budget, outage frequency and length, and how much of the house you want covered — the factors that most consistently separate a good portable fit from a good standby fit. Actual prices vary a lot by region, brand, and installation complexity; get two or three local quotes before you commit to a standby install.
